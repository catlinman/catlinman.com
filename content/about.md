Catlinman was the name I went by online from 2013 to 2020, and most people just called me Cat. I worked on pretty much anything a computer can do: digital art, animation and 3D modelling, programming, game design, web development and electronic music.

This site gathers what's left of that work. The gallery holds the cover art, animation and renders, the projects page covers the games, music visuals and communities, and the timeline below shows how it all fit together. If you want to talk about any of it, the contact page has everything you need.

<div class="callout">
I retired the Catlinman handle in 2020 and go by zealsprince now. Thanks for coming along for this part of the journey. To see what I'm up to these days, head over to <a href="https://zealsprince.com">zealsprince.com</a>.

# People & Places {#people}

A lot of this happened with other people, in places we built together.

- **[Hivecom](/projects/#hivecom)**: the gaming community I started with Jokler and Trif in 2013, and still around today.
- **[Tradrec](/projects/#tradrec)**: the electronic music label I co-founded in 2014 and ran from 2015.
- **[Mavulp](/projects/#mavulp)**: the development collective Jokler and I ran, home to Hivecom and our other projects.
- **[Nekodrop](/projects/#nekodrop)**: my own server network, which has since run its course.

Thanks to the artists I got to make things for: Bossfight, meganeko, nanobii, Chantaro, Jan Dolanský, Dex Arson, Sound Remedy, Noyire, Johann Regaya, Axis Defied and everyone else in the [gallery](/gallery/). The quotes you can unlock on the front page were written by friends: Trif, Jan Dolanský, Chantaro, Kagedreng and Mixilaxi.

I spent a lot of time making my tools look and feel right, and most of it ended up on GitHub under the neko name.

- **[neko-config](https://github.com/catlinman/neko-config)**: configuration files, themes and workspaces for just about everything I used, from Atom, Sublime Text and Blender to a TeamSpeak skin and icon pack, terminal setups and server configs. The command line dotfiles have since moved to [zealsprince/.dotfiles](https://github.com/zealsprince/.dotfiles).
- **[NekoRoX](https://github.com/catlinman/foobar2000)**: a foobar2000 theme forked from CaTRoX, with custom panels for synced lyrics, cover flow, Last.fm, Discord rich presence and YouTube playback. It's the most starred thing I made as Catlinman. These days I work on [rox](https://github.com/zealsprince/rox), a standalone player built to do everything NekoRoX did.
- **[Neko Atom UI](https://github.com/catlinman/neko-atom-ui)** and **[Neko Atom Syntax](https://github.com/catlinman/neko-atom-syntax)**: a contrasted dark theme for Atom with a customizable accent color, and a subtle syntax theme matched to it.
- **[Neko Fox](https://github.com/catlinman/neko-fox)**: a clean, focused Firefox theme that stays responsive for mouse and keyboard users alike, based on moamenk's minimal theme.
- **[Nekobeat](https://github.com/catlinman/neko-beat)**: an osu! skin covering every game mode.
- **[Neko Style](https://github.com/catlinman/neko-style)**: neko flavored website themes for Stylish and other CSS replacers.
- **[Hivecom TeamSpeak](https://github.com/catlinman/hivecom-teamspeak)**: the Hivecom take on my personal TeamSpeak theme.

One of the questions I got asked most was what tools I use, so I kept a full list of my hardware and every program in my workflow. It's preserved exactly as it stood in 2017.
